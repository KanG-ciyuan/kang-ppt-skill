## Keep / Adapt / Reject / Invent

### Keep

- audience and decision before layouts;
- source-aware, reproducible authoring;
- one message and narrative job per slide;
- evidence-aware claims;
- deterministic checks plus full-size visual review;
- repair and recheck after visible defects.

### Adapt

- require three readable, materially different directions only for major or explicitly high-aesthetic work;
- keep the chosen direction local to the current task;
- keep personal judgment rules separate from the installed implementation engine.

### Reject

- copied renderers, runtimes, template libraries, style catalogs, logos, or brand identity;
- one fixed font, palette, page structure, domain, or motion preset;
- thumbnail collages as final design proof;
- palette-only directions, decorative motion, and unsupported commercial claims.

### Invent

- proportional process-depth routing for major, ordinary, template, selective, conversion, and read-only tasks;
- `verified / historical / to_verify / do_not_publish` claim states;
- a motion-purpose test based on sequence, hierarchy, causality, comparison, or attention;
- a dual completion gate where a technical pass cannot override failed full-size visual review.

## Design Advantages

These are intentional design properties, not universal performance claims:

- **Companion boundary:** personal design judgment can evolve without forking the PPTX engine.
- **Process-depth routing:** major work earns direction exploration while small work stays direct.
- **Full-size direction rule:** readable representative slides replace thumbnail collage guessing.
- **Evidence boundary:** generated atmosphere, runtime proof, historical notes, and unsafe/private information remain distinct.
